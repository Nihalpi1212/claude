title: Is Pigment free?
description: Pigment is free and open source under the MIT or Apache 2.0 license, works offline, and its optional AI features use your own provider account.
order: 10
verified: 0.5.0
---

Yes. Pigment is free and open source. The code is licensed under MIT or Apache 2.0, your choice, and is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Nihalpi1212/claude/tree/claude/pigment-photocraft/pigment). It is based on [PhotoCraft](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft), which is under the same licenses.

Pigment works offline and makes no network request on its own. Its smart selection tools and **Layer › Remove Background** run on your computer, with no cloud and no account.

The generative AI features are optional. They use your own OpenAI or Google Gemini account, and the provider bills that account for every request. See [Use generative AI](../guides/use-generative-ai.md).

See [Install Pigment](../getting-started/install.md) to download it.
