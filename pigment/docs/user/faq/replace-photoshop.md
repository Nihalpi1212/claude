---
title: Can Pigment replace Photoshop?
description: Not yet. Pigment 0.5 is an alpha and not ready for daily professional work, but it follows Photoshop's menus and shortcuts and has about two thirds of its features.
order: 30
verified: 0.5.0
---

Not yet. Pigment 0.5 is an alpha. Measured against a list of 1,432 Photoshop features, about 907 are done, 304 are partial or work differently, 153 are missing and 68 cannot be built without Adobe's own services or models. "Done" means the feature is there, not that it is as fast or as reliable as in Photoshop.

The biggest gaps are PSD files that Photoshop does not yet always accept, speed with large documents and many layers, open bugs, brush feel, some Photoshop workspaces that are still plain dialogs, typography depth and plug-in compatibility. Pigment 0.4 had 83 features that 0.5 does not have yet, such as adjustment presets and paragraph typography settings; they are planned for 0.6.

What Pigment does well already: layers, masks, adjustment layers, layer styles, type, vector shapes, brushes and live-preview filters, real PSD files, and optional generative AI with your own account, in a menu and shortcut layout that follows Photoshop.

The [parity page](../../parity/README.md) has the numbers by area and the list of Pigment 0.4 features still to come.
