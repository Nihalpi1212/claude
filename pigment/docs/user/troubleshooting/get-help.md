Open **Help › Report an Issue…**. It takes you to [Pigment's issue tracker](https://github.com/Nihalpi1212/claude/issues) on GitHub, where you can ask a question or report a bug.

## What to include

1. What you did, step by step, and what you expected.
2. What happened instead. A screenshot or a short video helps.
3. Your system details. Choose **Help › System Info…**, click **Copy**, and paste the text into your message. It lists the Pigment version, your operating system and processor, and your graphics adapter.
4. The file, if one file causes it. Zip a PSD, `.pigment` or other file and attach it. Leave it out if it contains anything private.
5. For a generative AI problem: the feature, the provider (OpenAI or Google Gemini) and the model if you chose one. Never paste your API key.

## Before you post

Search the tracker for your problem first. If someone already reported it, add your details there instead of opening a new issue. For known problems, see the other pages in [Troubleshooting](index.md) and the known issues in the [release notes](../../RELEASE-NOTES-0.5.0.md#known-issues).

Pigment is based on PhotoCraft. A problem in the editor itself may already be known upstream: you can search [PhotoCraft's issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/issues) too, but report Pigment problems in Pigment's.
